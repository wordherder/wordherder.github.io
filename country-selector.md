# Country Selector

- **Role** — Product Owner
- **Stakeholders** — Business units (Digital), senior directors (Digital Commerce)
- **Team** — Cross-collaborative

**Overview:** TE Connectivity's website automatically selects a region for the customer based on their location, and they are shown one of four websites: English (US/Global), Chinese, Japanese, or German. The manual switch is located in the footer, which will present a problem when global commerce is fully rolled out.

## 1. The Problem

Once TE.com's commerce initiative went global, everything was impacted. Language was tabled in favor of solving the literal commerce problem — pricing and taxes. What currency do we show? How do we know when to show the euro versus the pound?

## 2. The Proposal

The answer was in the cookies. If the customer was new to the website, we decided to ask them to select their country. And for those who already had a cookie in their browser's cache, we would give them an easy way to change their country.

## 3. The Proposed Wireframe

![Wireframe of the site header with a Country dropdown added beside Sign In and Lists, opening a Select Your Country panel](images/country-selector-wireframe.png)

The country control moves from the footer into the global header, beside Sign In and Lists, and opens a "Select Your Country" panel with a View More link for the full list.

## 4. The End Product

![Live TE.com header showing a welcome banner identifying the visitor's country, with Choose Another Site and Stay On This Site options, and the full country picker modal](images/country-selector-live.png)

The shipped version greets the visitor with their detected country and two clear choices — *Choose Another Site* or *Stay On This Site* — backed by a searchable modal of popular locations and the full alphabetical country list.
