# Type Ahead Redesign

- **Role** — Product Owner
- **Stakeholders** — Business units (Digital), senior directors (Digital Commerce)
- **Team** — Cross-collaborative

**Overview:** Over the past 18 months, we've automated the type-ahead links and added more content. Most recently, we included part numbers in the auto-suggest. This is a great deal of data in a small space that has no consistent information organization.

## 1. The Problem

As a customer begins to type, a variety of suggestions appear in no particular formatted order. These range from product category names to content articles, and to the uninitiated it is difficult to discern which suggestion is going to be the most useful. The idea of "one click" to the customer's goal is good, but we still need to give them information about what they are clicking on.

## 2. The Hypothesis

The five guidelines of the Hierarchy of Needs are functionality, reliability, usability, proficiency, and creativity. By designing a drop-down that meets these guidelines, the customer will have a functional type-ahead that reliably presents information in an organized, easy-to-understand format, which will encourage them to use it frequently to find what they need.

## 3. The Goals

The primary objective is to provide an elegant, easy-to-use interface for the auto-suggest's type-ahead. The measures for this objective:

- increase in clicks on the type-ahead's suggestions
- increase in product interest KPI on the pages reached via the type-ahead suggestion
- decrease in re-searches during session
- decrease in bounce rate during session

## 4. The Quantitative Data

Currently, type-ahead clicks account for 2.4% of total search queries. Of that 2.4%, nearly 40% of the customers immediately exit the website without performing any other activity. Although 2.4% is a low number, the extraordinarily high abandonment rate raises red flags. The remaining 97.6% who do not use the type-ahead's auto-suggest either ignore it or have learned that it is not helpful in its current state.

## 5. The Usability Test Scenarios

![Type-ahead prototype for the query "cat", grouping results into Product Suggestions, Categories, Series, and Industries & Solutions, with a product preview panel on the right](images/typeahead-prototype.png)

The tester will show each participant the search box and the type-ahead's auto-suggest prototype, then ask the participant to find products using the type-ahead. The tester should encourage participants to talk through the steps they are taking and to speak up about any confusion that gives them cause for hesitation.

If a participant has difficulty figuring out where to begin, the tester may demonstrate how the auto-suggest works by typing the word *connectors* into the search box and then selecting an option.

## 6. Findings & Recommendation

Out of the 7 participants, all found the redesign to be an improvement upon the existing design.
