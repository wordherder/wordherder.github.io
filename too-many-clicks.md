# Too Many Clicks

- **Role** — Product Owner
- **Stakeholders** — Business units (Digital), senior directors (Digital Commerce)
- **Team** — Cross-collaborative

**Overview:** Of the multiple customer personas, two stood out as having a need to purchase products quickly: the engineer creating the prototype, and the purchasing manager. The goal was to reduce the number of clicks to the product details page.

## 1. The Problem

There are too many clicks between the beginning of a customer's search and the click where the customer adds the product to their cart. Much of this is due to content pages created for search engine optimization purposes: they serve as landing, or entry, pages but are not useful as intermediary steps between the search and the cart.

## 2. The Data

Quantitative data showed that **over 80% of searches** conducted on TE.com's website were part numbers.

Qualitative data confirmed this during usability sessions with engineers and purchasing managers. **Both of these customer personas worked off a list of part numbers** when they reached the point where they needed to place an order.

![Two click paths from the home page: Path A, typing in the search box, takes at least 3 clicks; Path B, category browse, takes at least 4 clicks. Both include an unbounded number of filter and sort clicks.](images/click-paths-a-b.png)

| Path | Route | Minimum clicks |
|---|---|---:|
| **A** | Type in the search box → listings → product → variants / add to cart | ≥ 3 |
| **B** | Category browse → landing → listings → product → variants / add to cart | ≥ 4 |

In both paths, any number of filters and sorts could be applied along the way — *N* additional clicks.

## 3. The Solution

Add the part numbers to the auto-suggest drop-down of the search box.

Until December 2018, only dictionary words were used in the type-ahead. By adding the part numbers and linking the click on the part number to the details page with the buy button, **the number of clicks was reduced to 1**.

![Shortest path: begin typing in the search box, select the suggested part number, and arrive at the product details page with variants filtered and Add to Cart available in a single click](images/click-path-shortest.png)
